International Student Hub

Everything non-EU students need about money, work, and finance in Ireland.

🛂
SUSI Eligibility
  • Resident in Ireland/EU/EEA/UK for 3 of the last 5 years? You may qualify
  • Refugee status or certain immigration permissions also qualify
  • EU/EEA only residency = fee grant only, not maintenance
  • Nationality alone doesn't decide — residency history does
⏰
Work Hours (non-EEA)
  • Term time: maximum 20 hours per week on student visa
  • Academic holidays: full-time work permitted
  • Exceeding 20hrs risks your visa — track with our Work Tracker
  • EU/EEA students have no work hour restrictions
💶
Visa Funds Requirement
  • Must show €10,000 accessible in your bank at visa application
  • Must be actual funds — not a letter of support
  • Keep 3–6 months of bank statements as evidence
  • Separate from course fees — you need both
🏦
Banking in Ireland
  • Revolut — instant setup, best for international transfers
  • N26 — free EU account, no foreign transaction fees
  • Bank of Ireland — free student account, widely accepted
  • Get your PPS number first — needed to work or open accounts
🎓
Scholarships
  • Govt of Ireland: €25,000/yr + €5,750 fees + research costs
  • TCD, UCD, DCU all have country-specific undergraduate awards
  • Many have Oct/Nov deadlines for the following academic year
  • Check your university's international office — many go unclaimed
💸
Irish Tax
  • Get your PPS number at mywelfare.ie or local Intreo office
  • Working? Same tax credits as Irish students — claim them
  • Started or finished mid-year? You're likely owed a refund
  • Claim via Revenue myAccount at revenue.ie — free to use

FAQ

Common questions

Answers to what students ask most.

Is StudentCent affiliated with SUSI or Revenue?▾
No. StudentCent is independent. Always verify final figures at susi.ie or revenue.ie.
How accurate is the SUSI calculator?▾
Very accurate — we use the published 2025/26 thresholds directly from SUSI. Use it as a strong estimate then confirm on susi.ie.
Can international students use StudentCent?▾
Yes — we have a dedicated hub covering SUSI eligibility for non-EU students, work permit hours, visa funds, Irish tax, and scholarships.
What is the SUSI holiday earnings limit?▾
SUSI allows students to earn up to €8,424 outside term time. Go over by even €1 and your grant can be cut. Our Work Tracker keeps you safe.
Can my parents claim the rent tax credit too?▾
Yes! If your parents pay your student rent they can each claim €1,000 — up to €2,000 for the household. Tell them!
Is Pro worth it?▾
