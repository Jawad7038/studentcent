What counts as "holiday earnings"?
Income earned outside term time while you were a student the previous year. Summer jobs, Christmas work, Easter shifts — all count. Income during term time is counted differently under the main household income assessment.
💡 Tip: Even going €1 over the limit can reduce your grant. If you're close, stop taking extra shifts or switch to term-time hours which are assessed differently.

Everything you need to know about studying in Ireland.
🛂 SUSI for international students
  • •Qualify if resident in Ireland/EU/EEA/UK for 3 of the last 5 years
  • •Refugee status or certain immigration permissions also qualify
  • •EU/EEA residency only = fee grant only, not maintenance
⏰ Work permit hours (non-EEA)
  • •Term time: maximum 20 hours per week
  • •Holidays: full-time work permitted
  • •Exceeding 20hrs risks your student visa — track carefully
💶 Visa funds requirement
  • •Must prove access to €10,000 at visa application time
  • •Must be in your bank account — not a letter of support
  • •Keep 3–6 months of bank statements as evidence
